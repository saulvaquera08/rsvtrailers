Texas Health Code Compliance

Operating a food trailer in Texas requires compliance with the Texas Department of State Health Services (DSHS) and your local county health department. RSV trailers are built with these requirements in mind:

  • NSF-rated equipment options for commercial food preparation
  • Stainless steel work surfaces and proper ventilation
  • Plumbing rough-ins for handwash and 3-compartment sinks
  • Grease trap compatibility and proper drainage
  • Electrical systems designed for commercial equipment loads

While permits and final inspections depend on your specific city and county, we guide you through what to expect so there are no surprises.
