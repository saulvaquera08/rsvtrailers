Florida DBPR Compliance

Operating a food trailer in Florida requires a license from the Division of Hotels and Restaurants (DBPR) and compliance with Florida's food safety regulations. RSV trailers are built with Florida compliance in mind:

  • Commercial-grade ventilation hood infrastructure
  • Plumbing for handwash sink and 3-compartment warewashing sink
  • Proper hot and cold holding equipment options
  • Fire suppression system available ($3,000 USD add-on)
  • Stainless steel food-contact surfaces and smooth, cleanable finishes
  • Adequate lighting and pest-control design features

We help guide you through Florida's licensing process so you understand what DBPR requires before your pre-operational inspection.
